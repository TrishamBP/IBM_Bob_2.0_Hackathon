# ACME Corp Mission, History, and Values

This document explains *why* ACME exists, the brief fictional history of how we got here, and the values we expect every employee to apply to their day-to-day decisions. These are not posters — they are the basis for hiring, performance, and promotion decisions across the company.

## Mission

> To make enterprise software that people actually want to use, by combining the rigor of large-scale cloud platforms with the usability of consumer products and the safety of regulated industries.

We exist because enterprise software, in our view, has historically asked too much of users. ACME's mission is to compress the distance between intent and outcome — for end users, for developers, and for the operators who run our platforms. Every product decision at ACME should be traceable back to this mission.

## Vision

By 2030 (in this fictional universe), we want ACME to be the default enterprise platform that organizations choose when they need both usability and regulated-industry rigor. That means our products must work for the largest enterprises in the world *without* requiring their employees to be experts in our platform.

## Values

ACME has five values. They are deliberately short so that they can be cited in design reviews, hiring debriefs, and difficult decisions.

### 1. Curiosity

We default to asking why before we default to building. A pull request without context is a missed opportunity. We reward engineers, designers, and PMs who dig one level deeper than was asked of them.

### 2. Ownership

You own the outcome, not the task. If your change breaks production, you stay engaged until it is fixed — even if the underlying system is owned by another team. We do not hand off responsibility by handing off a ticket.

### 3. Rigor

We measure before we optimize. We write tests before we ship. We document before we onboard. We treat rigor as a form of respect for the next person to touch our work.

### 4. Candor

We say what we think, kindly. We give and receive direct feedback in real time, not at performance review season. We disagree in the room and commit outside it.

### 5. Care

We treat colleagues, customers, and the people affected by our software as people first. We design for accessibility. We respect time zones. We assume good intent and verify before we judge.

## How Values Show Up

Values at ACME are not abstract. They are explicitly evaluated in:

- **Hiring debriefs** — interviewers cite specific examples tied to a value.
- **Performance reviews** — see [`01-hr/performance-review-policy.md`](../01-hr/performance-review-policy.md).
- **Promotion packets** — promotion requires demonstrated impact across at least three of the five values.
- **Engineering design reviews** — designs are evaluated against rigor and care, not just feasibility.
- **Customer escalations** — ownership and candor are the basis for how we communicate during incidents.

If you observe behavior that contradicts these values, you are encouraged to raise it. See [`01-hr/employee-grievance-procedure.md`](../01-hr/employee-grievance-procedure.md) and [`01-hr/code-of-conduct.md`](../01-hr/code-of-conduct.md) for the channels.
