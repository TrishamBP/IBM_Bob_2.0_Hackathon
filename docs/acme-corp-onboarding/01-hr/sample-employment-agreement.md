# ACME Corp Sample Employment Agreement

> **FICTIONAL EXAMPLE — NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** This is a sample employment agreement template provided for demonstration and onboarding-portal testing only. It does not constitute legal advice and must not be used in any real-world employment relationship without review and customization by qualified legal counsel admitted to the relevant jurisdiction. ACME Corp itself is fictional. The clauses below are illustrative only.

## Purpose

This document is a **sample employment agreement template** that ACME's (fictional) HR Operations team uses as a starting point. The actual executed agreement is issued by Legal and Compliance through the HR system; this template is shared in the onboarding library so that new hires can review the structure in advance.

## Template

**EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT**

This Employment Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into on **[YYYY-MM-DD]** (the "Effective Date") by and between:

**ACME Corporation Pvt. Ltd.**, a company incorporated under the laws of India, having its registered office at ACME Tower, Hitec City, Hyderabad, Telangana 500081, India (the "Company"),

and

**[Employee Full Name]**, an individual residing at **[Employee Address]** (the "Employee").

### 1. Position and Duties

1.1 The Company agrees to employ the Employee in the position of **[Job Title]**, reporting to **[Manager Name], [Manager Title]**.

1.2 The Employee's primary work location shall be **[Office or "Remote — [City, Country]"]**. The Employee may be required to travel to other Company offices or to customer sites as reasonably necessary.

1.3 The Employee shall perform the duties assigned to them diligently, in good faith, and to the best of their abilities, in compliance with all Company policies (see [`00-company/employee-handbook.md`](../00-company/employee-handbook.md)).

### 2. Effective Date and Probation

2.1 The Employee's employment shall commence on **[YYYY-MM-DD]** (the "Start Date").

2.2 The first six (6) months of employment shall be a probationary period. See [`01-hr/probation-and-confirmation-process.md`](./probation-and-confirmation-process.md).

### 3. Compensation

3.1 The Company shall pay the Employee a gross annual salary of **INR [Amount] / USD [Amount] / GBP [Amount]**, payable in arrears in twelve (12) equal monthly installments, subject to applicable tax withholding.

3.2 The Employee shall be eligible for the Company's discretionary annual bonus plan, target **[X]% of base salary**, subject to Company and individual performance. See [`01-hr/performance-review-policy.md`](./performance-review-policy.md).

3.3 The Employee shall be eligible for the Company's standard benefits program. See [`01-hr/benefits-enrollment-form.md`](./benefits-enrollment-form.md).

### 4. Working Hours

4.1 The Employee's standard working hours shall be **40 hours per week**. Core collaboration hours are defined in [`00-company/office-locations-and-working-arrangements.md`](../00-company/office-locations-and-working-arrangements.md).

4.2 The Employee shall be entitled to **[X] days of paid time off per year**, in accordance with [`01-hr/leave-and-attendance-policy.md`](./leave-and-attendance-policy.md).

### 5. Confidentiality

5.1 The Employee shall not, during or after employment, disclose any Confidential Information of the Company or its customers, except as required by law or with the Company's written consent. See [`03-security/confidentiality-agreement.md`](../03-security/confidentiality-agreement.md) and [`03-security/privacy-acknowledgement.md`](../03-security/privacy-acknowledgement.md).

### 6. Intellectual Property

6.1 All inventions, discoveries, and works of authorship created by the Employee within the scope of employment (or using Company resources) shall be the sole property of the Company.

### 7. Non-Solicitation and Non-Compete

7.1 For the duration of employment and for **[X] months** after termination, the Employee shall not solicit any of the Company's employees or customers.

7.2 Non-compete obligations, where enforceable under applicable law, are limited to the scope and duration permitted by such law.

### 8. Termination

8.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement in accordance with applicable law and the Company's separation policy. See [`01-hr/employee-separation-policy.md`](./employee-separation-policy.md).

### 9. Governing Law

9.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India (for India-based employees) / England and Wales (for UK-based employees) / the State of Washington (for US-based employees). Disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts in the applicable jurisdiction.

### 10. Acknowledgement

10.1 The Employee acknowledges that they have read and understood this Agreement, the [`01-hr/code-of-conduct.md`](./code-of-conduct.md), and the [`00-company/employee-handbook.md`](../00-company/employee-handbook.md), and agree to comply with them.

**Date:** [YYYY-MM-DD]

**Signed by the Employee:**
