# HR Frequently Asked Questions

This FAQ answers the most common HR questions asked by ACME Corp employees across our Hyderabad, Bengaluru, London, and Seattle offices. Topics cover pay, benefits, leave, probation, performance, working arrangements, reimbursements, and conduct. For anything not covered here, reach out to your HRBP listed in [../09-contacts/contact-directory.md](../09-contacts/contact-directory.md) or email Priya Nair (priya.nair@acme.example), HR Onboarding Coordinator.

### Q: When do I get paid?
ACME Corp pays salaried employees on the last working day of each calendar month, with the funds landing in your registered bank account by end-of-business that day. For new joiners, your first paycheck may be pro-rated based on your start date and is processed through the Payroll system maintained by Sanjay Patel's team. Make sure your [bank details](../01-hr/payroll-and-bank-information-form.md) and [tax declarations](../01-hr/tax-declaration-form.md) are submitted before the payroll cut-off (the 20th of each month) to avoid delays.

### Q: How do I enroll in benefits?
New hires have a 14-day enrollment window from their start date to elect benefits such as health insurance, provident fund, and gratuity through the [benefits enrollment form](../01-hr/benefits-enrollment-form.md) on hr.acme.example. Outside that window, changes are only allowed during open enrollment or after a qualifying life event (marriage, birth, etc.). Contact Rohit Sharma (rohit.sharma@acme.example), Benefits Specialist, if you need help choosing plans or uploading supporting documents.

### Q: When is open enrollment?
Open enrollment runs during the first two weeks of April each year, with elected changes effective from May 1. You will receive a calendar invitation and a portal notification on hr.acme.example at least 30 days before the window opens. The [employee handbook](../00-company/employee-handbook.md) lists the full schedule and the qualifying life events that allow off-cycle changes.

### Q: How do I request leave?
Submit all leave requests through the Leave module on hr.acme.example at least three business days in advance, with manager approval required before the time off is confirmed. Sick leave of more than two consecutive days requires a doctor's certificate uploaded to the same portal. See [../01-hr/leave-and-attendance-policy.md](../01-hr/leave-and-attendance-policy.md) for accrual rates, carry-forward rules, and the encashment policy at separation.

### Q: What is the probation period?
The standard probation period at ACME Corp is six months for individual contributors and nine months for people managers, starting from your joining date. During probation, either party may end employment with two weeks' notice; benefits like PF and gratuity still accrue during this time. The full confirmation criteria and process are documented in [../01-hr/probation-and-confirmation-process.md](../01-hr/probation-and-confirmation-process.md).

### Q: How do I update my bank info?
Use the [payroll and bank information form](../01-hr/payroll-and-bank-information-form.md) on hr.acme.example to update your account number, IFSC, or bank name — changes submitted before the 20th of the month take effect from that month's payroll. You must upload a cancelled cheque or a bank-issued letter as proof; HR will reject forms that don't include the verification document. Reach out to Sanjay Patel (sanjay.patel@acme.example), Payroll Lead, if the form is stuck in approval.

### Q: How do I report a grievance?
Grievances can be filed through the Grievance module on hr.acme.example or by emailing your HRBP directly; both channels are confidential and acknowledged within two business days. For sensitive matters involving harassment or discrimination, please follow the [employee grievance procedure](../01-hr/employee-grievance-procedure.md) which guarantees a 10-business-day investigation timeline. You may also escalate directly to Ananya Sharma (ananya.sharma@acme.example), VP HR, if you feel your concern is not being addressed.

### Q: What happens during performance review?
ACME runs two formal review cycles per year — mid-year in April and year-end in October — combining self-assessment, peer feedback, and a calibration session led by your skip-level. Your manager will share the rating rubric and goal-setting template at least four weeks before the review window opens; ratings map to bonus payouts and merit increases effective the following January. See [../01-hr/performance-review-policy.md](../01-hr/performance-review-policy.md) for the rating scale, calibration rules, and the appeals process.

### Q: How do I change my working model (hybrid, remote, office-based)?
Changes to your working model require your manager's approval and an updated [remote and hybrid working agreement](../01-hr/remote-and-hybrid-working-policy.md) filed with HR. Remote-first arrangements also require an IT home-office equipment check (see [../02-it/equipment-replacement.md](../02-it/equipment-replacement.md)) and a security attestation that your home network meets ACME standards. The current allowed models — Hybrid (3 days in office), Remote-India, and Remote-International — are described in [../00-company/office-locations-and-working-arrangements.md](../00-company/office-locations-and-working-arrangements.md).

### Q: Can I bring my own device (BYOD) for work?
ACME allows BYOD for smartphones only, enrolled via Microsoft Intune; laptops and workstations must be corporate-issued. Your personal phone, once enrolled, will have a separate work profile and ACME cannot view your personal apps or photos. Review [../03-security/byod-policy.md](../03-security/byod-policy.md) for the privacy boundaries, mandatory encryption, and the remote-wipe conditions that apply if the device is lost.

### Q: How does parental leave work?
Birthing parents receive 26 weeks of paid maternity leave as per Indian statutory requirement, and non-birthing parents receive four weeks of paid paternity leave, both of which can be combined with accrued leaves. ACME additionally offers a 12-week phased-return program where new parents can work 50% hours at full pay after the statutory leave ends. Submit your request through the Leave module on hr.acme.example along with the medical certificate or birth registration; the [leave and attendance policy](../01-hr/leave-and-attendance-policy.md) covers the full eligibility and documentation matrix.

### Q: When do I get confirmed after probation?
Your manager initiates the confirmation review 30 days before your probation end date, and the final confirmation letter is issued within 15 days of probation completion if all criteria are met. Confirmation triggers eligibility for full bonus accrual, gratuity vesting, and the next tier of paid leaves. The full criteria — including the minimum rating, mandatory training completion, and manager + skip-level approval — are in [../01-hr/probation-and-confirmation-process.md](../01-hr/probation-and-confirmation-process.md).

### Q: How do I update my address?
Update your residential address on the [employee information form](../01-hr/employee-information-form.md) on hr.acme.example and upload a proof of address (Aadhaar, utility bill, or rental agreement not older than 90 days). Address changes may trigger a payroll state review for L1/L2 employees who have moved between Indian states. If the change affects your tax residency, also update the [tax declaration form](../01-hr/tax-declaration-form.md) before the next payroll cut-off.

### Q: How do I get reimbursed for travel and other expenses?
Submit expense claims through the Expense module on hr.acme.example within 30 days of incurring the expense, with itemized receipts and the business purpose noted. Approvals flow through your manager first, then Sanjay Patel's payroll team; reimbursements are paid out with the next monthly payroll cycle. The travel entitlements, daily allowance rates, and per-office hotel caps are listed in [../01-hr/employee-information-form.md](../01-hr/employee-information-form.md) and in the [employee handbook](../00-company/employee-handbook.md).

### Q: What is the dress code?
ACME follows a business-casual dress code in all four offices — collared shirts, blouses, kurtas, chinos, or smart jeans are all acceptable, with no requirement for ties or formal suits except for client-facing meetings. Footwear must be closed-toe in lab and data-center areas for safety reasons. The [code of conduct](../01-hr/code-of-conduct.md) covers dress expectations during external events and customer visits.

### Q: Where can I find the employee handbook?
The [employee handbook](../00-company/employee-handbook.md) lives on the HR section of wiki.acme.example and is also linked from the portal.acme.example home page under "People". It consolidates policies on conduct, leave, attendance, benefits, and grievances in a single 80-page reference. You will receive an acknowledgment task to confirm you have read it during your first week — see [../07-workflows/first-week-onboarding.md](../07-workflows/first-week-onboarding.md) for the due date.
