# ACME Corp Employee Handbook

The Employee Handbook is your single-page reference for the policies that apply to every ACME employee. It is intentionally short — every policy below has a dedicated document that goes into detail. Use this page as the index; follow the links for specifics.

> This is a fictional handbook. It is not legal advice. Any real-world employment, tax, or compliance decision requires qualified professional review.
