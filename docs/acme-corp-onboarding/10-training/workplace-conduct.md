# Workplace Conduct Training (ACME-TRN-009)

> Fictional document. All systems, links, and addresses are illustrative.

## 1. Module Overview

Workplace Conduct Training is the mandatory curriculum covering ACME Corp's code of conduct, anti-harassment policy, grievance procedure, communication guidelines, hybrid working model, and leave policy. It is required of every employee regardless of role, level, or office, and it gates the final HR sign-off in the onboarding workflow. The module grounds every employee in the standards of professional behaviour at ACME, the channels for raising concerns, and the operational rules of hybrid work and time-off.

The module is owned by HR Operations (VP HR Ananya Sharma, `ananya.sharma@acme.example`) and coordinated by Anjali Iyer (Training Operations, `anjali.iyer@acme.example`), with content co-owned by the HRBP trio (Kavya Krishnan for Cloud/Workspace, Deepika Rao for Intelligence/QE/Support, Sanjay Patel for Platform/Sales).

## 2. Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, the learner will be able to:

1. Recall the ACME code of conduct's five commitments and identify the situations they apply to.
2. Define harassment per the ACME anti-harassment policy, recognise examples, and execute the reporting workflow.
3. Describe the grievance procedure stages (informal, formal, escalation) and the timelines for each.
4. Apply the ACME communication guidelines across email, chat, video, and documentation channels.
5. Summarise the hybrid working model: office days, remote days, equipment expectations, and travel rules.
6. Identify the leave categories, the leave-request workflow, and the no-surprise rule for planned absences.

## 6. Outline of Topics Covered

### Block A — Code of Conduct (25 min)

- The five ACME commitments: respect, integrity, accountability, inclusion, stewardship.
- Conduct in person, online, and at customer/partner sites.
- Conflicts of interest, gifts and entertainment, financial interests.
- Reference: [`../01-hr/code-of-conduct.md`](../01-hr/code-of-conduct.md).

### Block B — Anti-Harassment Policy (25 min)

- Definition of harassment: sexual, gender-based, caste-based, race-based, disability-based, age-based, and other protected categories.
- Examples of prohibited conduct; what "hostile work environment" means in ACME's context.
- Reporting channels: HRBP, HR Operations (`hr-ops@acme.example`), anonymous hotline (`helpdesk.acme.example`), or VP HR directly.
- No-retaliation guarantee; interim protections during investigation.
- Reference: [`../01-hr/anti-harassment-policy.md`](../01-hr/anti-harassment-policy.md).

### Block C — Grievance Procedure (20 min)

- Three stages: informal resolution, formal complaint, escalation to HR Operations / VP HR.
- Timelines: acknowledgement within 48 hours; investigation within 10 business days; written outcome within 20 business days.
- Roles: HRBP (intake), HR Operations (formal stage), VP HR (escalation), legal counsel where applicable.
- Reference: [`../01-hr/employee-grievance-procedure.md`](../01-hr/employee-grievance-procedure.md).

### Block D — Communication Guidelines (20 min)

- Channels: email (Outlook), chat (Teams), video (Teams meetings), documentation (`wiki.acme.example`), code (PR comments).
- Norms: response-time expectations, no-after-hours expectation, time-zone etiquette.
- Public communication: social media, conference talks, customer-facing content.
- Reference: [`../00-company/communication-guidelines.md`](../00-company/communication-guidelines.md).

### Block E — Hybrid Working Model (15 min)

- Office footprint: Hyderabad (HQ), Bengaluru, London, Seattle.
- Default rhythm: 3 office days, 2 remote days; team-level variations documented in team onboarding guides.
- Equipment expectations for home offices; reimbursement policy.
- Travel and cross-office visits.
- Reference: [`../01-hr/remote-and-hybrid-working-policy.md`](../01-hr/remote-and-hybrid-working-policy.md) and [`../00-company/office-locations-and-working-arrangements.md`](../00-company/office-locations-and-working-arrangements.md).

### Block F — Leave Policy & Workflow (10 min)

- Leave categories: annual, sick, casual, parental, bereavement, sabbatical, unpaid.
- Request workflow: `hr.acme.example` → manager approval → calendar sync.
- The "no-surprise" rule: planned absences ≥ 3 days require at least 5 business days' notice.
- Reference: [`../01-hr/leave-and-attendance-policy.md`](../01-hr/leave-and-attendance-policy.md).

### Block G — Acknowledgement (5 min)

- Acknowledge the code of conduct and the anti-harassment policy.
- Acknowledge the grievance procedure and the no-retaliation commitment.
- Record the acknowledgement via [`../08-forms/policy-acknowledgement.md`](../08-forms/policy-acknowledgement.md).
