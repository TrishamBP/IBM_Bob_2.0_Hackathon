# ACME Corp Welcome Letter

> This is a fictional welcome letter template. It is for demonstration only. Any real-world offer communication requires review by qualified HR and legal professionals.

## Purpose

This document is the standard welcome letter sent to a new hire between offer acceptance and start date. It accompanies the formal offer letter (issued separately through the HR system) and is intended to make the new hire feel welcomed and oriented before day one.

## Sample Welcome Letter

**From:** Ananya Sharma, VP Human Resources, ACME Corp
**To:** [New Hire Full Name]
**Date:** 2026-09-01
**Subject:** Welcome to ACME Corp — your start date is confirmed

Dear [New Hire First Name],

Welcome to ACME Corp. We are delighted to confirm your start date as Monday, 2026-09-15. This letter is informal — your formal offer and terms of employment have already been issued separately through the HR system — and is intended to help you arrive on day one already oriented.

**A few things to know before you start:**

- **Your hiring manager** is [Manager Name]. They will meet you at 10:00 IST on your first day via Microsoft Teams.
- **Your onboarding buddy** is [Buddy Name], a peer from your team. They will reach out before day one.
- **Your HRBP** is [HRBP Name]. They are your escalation point for any HR matter.
- **Your laptop** will be ready for collection or couriered to your address. See [`02-it/laptop-and-workstation-allocation.md`](../02-it/laptop-and-workstation-allocation.md) for the standard configuration.
- **Your corporate identity** (`@acme.example` email) will be activated at 09:00 IST on your start date. See [`02-it/microsoft-365-account-activation.md`](../02-it/microsoft-365-account-activation.md).

**Three things to do before day one:**

1. Complete the [`08-forms/new-employee-information.md`](../08-forms/new-employee-information.md) and [`08-forms/emergency-contact.md`](../08-forms/emergency-contact.md) forms and return them to your HRBP at least 3 business days before your start date.
2. Review the [`00-company/welcome-to-acme.md`](../00-company/welcome-to-acme.md) and [`07-workflows/preboarding.md`](../07-workflows/preboarding.md).
3. If you are joining a hybrid office, plan to be on-site on day one — see [`00-company/office-locations-and-working-arrangements.md`](../00-company/office-locations-and-working-arrangements.md) for your office address.

**What to expect on day one:**

Your day will begin at 09:30 IST with an HR welcome session and end at 17:00 IST. The detailed agenda is in [`07-workflows/first-day-onboarding.md`](../07-workflows/first-day-onboarding.md). Expect to:

- Activate your Microsoft 365 account and set up MFA.
- Attend an HR orientation and benefits briefing.
- Have lunch with your buddy and team.
- Begin your role-specific onboarding (see [`05-teams/`](../05-teams/)).

We are glad you are joining us. If you have any question before day one, reach out to your HRBP or to me directly at `ananya.sharma@acme.example`.

Warmly,
**Ananya Sharma**
VP Human Resources, ACME Corp
