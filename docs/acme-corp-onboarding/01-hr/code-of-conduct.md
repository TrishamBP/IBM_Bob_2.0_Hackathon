# ACME Corp Code of Conduct

The Code of Conduct describes the standards of behavior expected of every ACME employee, contractor, and representative. It is binding on anyone acting on ACME's behalf and is referenced in the [`01-hr/sample-employment-agreement.md`](./sample-employment-agreement.md). This is a fictional policy and not legal advice.

## Purpose

A 3,500-person company cannot enumerate every ethical decision an employee will face. The Code of Conduct sets the floor — the minimum standards — and points employees to the values in [`00-company/mission-and-values.md`](../00-company/mission-and-values.md) when judgment is required.

## Core Standards

### 1. Integrity

- We tell the truth. We do not misrepresent product capabilities, customer commitments, financial metrics, or our own work.
- We do not accept bribes, kickbacks, or improper gifts. The gift policy permits nominal hospitality (up to INR 2,000 / USD 25 / GBP 20 per occurrence, max INR 10,000 / USD 125 / GBP 100 per year from any one party). Anything above this requires disclosure to the manager and Legal.
- We do not engage in conflicts of interest. Disclosure is required for any outside employment, board seat, or material financial interest in a vendor, customer, or competitor.

### 2. Respect

- We treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect regardless of background, identity, or role. See [`01-hr/anti-harassment-policy.md`](./anti-harassment-policy.md).
- We do not tolerate harassment, discrimination, bullying, or retaliation.
- We respect time zones and personal time. See [`00-company/communication-guidelines.md`](../00-company/communication-guidelines.md).

### 3. Confidentiality

- We protect ACME's and customers' Confidential Information. See [`03-security/confidentiality-agreement.md`](../03-security/confidentiality-agreement.md) and [`03-security/customer-data-handling.md`](../03-security/customer-data-handling.md).
- We do not discuss customers, internal metrics, or product plans outside ACME, including on social media.
- We do not trade in securities of customers, vendors, or partners based on Confidential Information.

### 4. Compliance

- We comply with applicable laws and regulations in every jurisdiction in which we operate.
- We follow all ACME policies, including security ([`03-security/`](../03-security/)), IT ([`02-it/`](../02-it/)), and HR ([`01-hr/`](./)) policies.
- We do not retaliate against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

### 5. Customer and Partner Conduct

- We do not make commitments to customers or partners beyond our role. Sales commitments require Sales approval; product roadmap commitments require Product Management approval. See [`00-company/communication-guidelines.md`](../00-company/communication-guidelines.md).
- We do not accept customer or partner gifts that would create even the appearance of a conflict of interest.

### 6. Asset Use

- We use ACME assets (laptops, network, software, time) for legitimate business purposes. Limited personal use (e.g., checking personal email during a break) is acceptable; systematic personal use, side-business use, or use for any unlawful purpose is not.
- We do not install unapproved software on ACME devices. See [`02-it/approved-software-installation.md`](../02-it/approved-software-installation.md).

### 7. AI Tool Use

- We use AI tools only in accordance with [`03-security/ai-tool-acceptable-use.md`](../03-security/ai-tool-acceptable-use.md).
- We do not enter customer data, Confidential Information, or source code into AI tools that are not approved for that data classification.
- We review AI-generated output before relying on it.

## Reporting Concerns

Employees who observe or suspect a violation of this Code should report it through one of these channels:

1. Their manager (if the issue is not about the manager).
2. Their HRBP (see [`09-contacts/contact-directory.md`](../09-contacts/contact-directory.md)).
3. Legal and Compliance (`hemant.joshi@acme.example`).
4. The anonymous reporting channel: `speakup@acme.example` (monitored by Legal).

See [`01-hr/employee-grievance-procedure.md`](./employee-grievance-procedure.md) for the full grievance process. ACME does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Acknowledgement

Every employee must acknowledge the Code of Conduct annually via [`08-forms/policy-acknowledgement.md`](../08-forms/policy-acknowledgement.md). New hires must acknowledge it within their first week — see [`07-workflows/policy-acknowledgement.md`](../07-workflows/policy-acknowledgement.md).
