# ACME Corp Performance Review Policy

This policy describes ACME's performance review cadence, rating scale, and the link between performance, compensation, and promotion. This is a fictional policy.

## Cadence

| Cycle | Purpose | Timing |
|-------|---------|--------|
| Quarterly check-in | Goal progress, blockers, calibration | End of each quarter |
| Mid-year review | Half-year rating, course correction | July |
| Annual review | Full-year rating, compensation, promotion | January |
| 30/60/90-day onboarding reviews | Probation track | Within first 90 days — see [`07-workflows/first-90-days.md`](../07-workflows/first-90-days.md) and [`01-hr/probation-and-confirmation-process.md`](./probation-and-confirmation-process.md) |

## Rating Scale

ACME uses a 5-point scale, applied quarterly and rolled up annually.

| Rating | Label | Indicative distribution |
|--------|-------|-----------------------------|
| 5 | Exceptional | ~5% |
| 4 | Exceeds expectations | ~20% |
| 3 | Meets expectations | ~65% |
| 2 | Partially meets expectations | ~8% |
| 1 | Does not meet expectations | ~2% |

Ratings are calibrated within and across teams to avoid rating inflation. The calibration meeting is chaired by the VP HR and the relevant business leader (CTO, CPO, COO).

## Goals

- Each employee maintains 3–5 quarterly goals, aligned with team OKRs.
- Goals are written in the HR system by week 2 of the quarter and approved by the manager.
- Mid-quarter adjustments are allowed for changing priorities.

## Compensation Link

- Annual base salary increases are tied to annual rating, with separate budgets for each rating tier.
- Annual bonus payouts are tied to company performance (60%) and individual rating (40%).
- Promotions are tied to demonstrated impact across at least three of the five values in [`00-company/mission-and-values.md`](../00-company/mission-and-values.md), not just to time-in-grade or rating.

## 1:1s

- Every employee has a weekly 30-minute 1:1 with their direct manager.
- 1:1s are the primary channel for ongoing feedback. They are not status updates.
- The employee owns the agenda; the manager owns the follow-up.

## Feedback Culture

- Feedback is expected to be specific, timely, and kind. See [`00-company/communication-guidelines.md`](../00-company/communication-guidelines.md).
- 360-degree feedback is collected for annual reviews for employees rated 3 or above.
- For employees rated 1 or 2, a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) is initiated.

## Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)

- A PIP is a 60-day structured plan with weekly check-ins, clear deliverables, and a written success criterion.
- A PIP is initiated with HRBP concurrence and is communicated in writing to the employee.
- Successful completion returns the employee to normal review cadence.
- Unsuccessful completion may lead to separation per [`01-hr/employee-separation-policy.md`](./employee-separation-policy.md).

## Acknowledgement

Managers acknowledge this policy annually. New hires are introduced to it during their first-week HR orientation.
