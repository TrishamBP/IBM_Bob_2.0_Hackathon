# ACME Corp Leave and Attendance Policy

This policy defines leave entitlements, attendance expectations, and the process for requesting, approving, and recording leave at ACME Corp. It is a fictional policy and not legal advice.

## Working Hours

- Standard working hours are 40 hours per week, Monday–Friday.
- Core collaboration hours are defined in [`00-company/office-locations-and-working-arrangements.md`](../00-company/office-locations-and-working-arrangements.md).
- Hybrid employees are expected in-office a minimum of two days per week. See [`01-hr/remote-and-hybrid-working-policy.md`](./remote-and-hybrid-working-policy.md).
- Remote employees are expected to be reachable during core hours and to publish their working hours in Outlook.

## Leave Categories

| Category | Entitlement (annual) | Carry-over | Cash-out on separation |
|----------|-----------------------|------------|--------------------------|
| Paid Time Off (PTO) | 25 days (India) / 25 days (US) / 28 days (UK) | Up to 10 days into next year | Yes, accrued and unused |
| Sick leave | 12 days | No carry-over | No |
| Casual leave | 5 days | No carry-over | No |
| Public holidays | Per office country | — | — |
| Parental leave — primary caregiver | 26 weeks paid | — | — |
| Parental leave — secondary caregiver | 12 weeks paid | — | — |
| Bereavement leave | 5 days per occurrence | — | — |
| Marriage leave | 5 days (per marriage) | — | — |
| Sabbatical (after 3 years) | Up to 4 weeks unpaid | — | — |

## Requesting Leave

1. Submit a request via ACME HR Hub → Leave. The request goes to the employee's manager for approval.
2. PTO requests of 5 days or more require 2 weeks' notice.
3. Sick leave can be requested same-day. Sick leave of more than 3 consecutive days requires a medical certificate.
4. Parental leave requires 8 weeks' notice where possible, with formal approval by the HR Director.
5. Bereavement and marriage leave are approved on submission; HR Operations validates the eligibility.

## Approval Workflow

| Request type | Approver 1 | Approver 2 |
|---------------|------------|------------|
| PTO ≤ 5 days | Manager | — |
| PTO > 5 days | Manager | HRBP |
| Sick leave ≤ 3 days | Manager (auto-approved) | — |
| Sick leave > 3 days | Manager | HRBP (with medical certificate) |
| Parental leave | Manager | HR Director |
| Bereavement | Manager (auto-approved) | — |
| Sabbatical | Manager | VP-level |

## Recording Attendance

- Attendance is recorded via Microsoft Teams presence and Outlook calendar, not via a biometric or badge-in system (per [`03-security/acceptable-use-policy.md`](../03-security/acceptable-use-policy.md) privacy sections).
- Engineers on on-call rotations log their availability via the on-call tool (see [`04-engineering/incident-response-and-on-call-introduction.md`](../04-engineering/practices/incident-response-and-on-call-introduction.md)).
- Managers review attendance as part of monthly 1:1s.

## Leave Without Pay (LWOP)

- LWOP requires manager and HRBP approval.
- LWOP of more than 30 days requires HR Director approval and triggers a review of benefits continuity.
- LWOP of more than 90 days triggers a separation review unless covered by a documented medical or family situation.

## Absence Without Intimation

- Absence without notification for 1 business day triggers a manager follow-up.
- Absence without notification for 3 business days triggers an HRBP outreach and may, after due process, be treated as job abandonment per applicable law.
- This provision is governed by the [`01-hr/employee-separation-policy.md`](./employee-separation-policy.md) and applicable law; nothing here overrides statutory protections.
