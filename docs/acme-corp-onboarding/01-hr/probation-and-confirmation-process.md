# ACME Corp Probation and Confirmation Process

This process governs the probationary period for new hires at ACME Corp and the formal confirmation of employment at the end of probation. This is a fictional process.

## Probationary Period

- The probationary period is **6 months** from the start date for all full-time employees.
- The probation end date is recorded in the employee's HR record (see [`01-hr/employee-information-form.md`](./employee-information-form.md)).
- Probation may be extended by up to 3 months in cases of documented performance concerns. Extension requires HR Director approval and is communicated in writing at least 2 weeks before the probation end date.

## Probation Reviews

| Review | Owner | Timing | Purpose |
|--------|-------|--------|---------|
| 30-day review | Hiring manager | Within first 30 days | Confirm fit, surface blockers, validate onboarding progress |
| 60-day review | Hiring manager | Within first 60 days | Confirm capability to perform at the role's expected level |
| 90-day review | Hiring manager + HRBP | Within first 90 days | Confirm capability and engagement; surface any concerns |
| Final probation review | Hiring manager + HRBP | By probation end date | Confirm employment or initiate separation |

See also [`07-workflows/first-30-days.md`](../07-workflows/first-30-days.md), [`07-workflows/first-60-days.md`](../07-workflows/first-60-days.md), and [`07-workflows/first-90-days.md`](../07-workflows/first-90-days.md).

## Confirmation

- If the final probation review is positive, the HRBP confirms employment in writing and updates the HR record.
- Confirmation triggers eligibility for:
  - Annual bonus plan participation.
  - Full PTO accrual rate.
  - Internal mobility (role changes within ACME).

## Non-Confirmation

- If the final probation review is negative, the HRBP and hiring manager meet with the employee to discuss either an extension (with documented improvement plan) or separation.
- Separation during probation follows the [`01-hr/employee-separation-policy.md`](./employee-separation-policy.md) and applicable law. Notice and severance terms in the employment agreement apply.

## Probation Performance Standards

The probation standards are explicit and role-specific. For engineering roles, the standards include:

- Successful setup of the development environment (see [`04-engineering/developer-workstation-setup.md`](../04-engineering/developer-workstation-setup.md)).
- Submission of at least one merged pull request to the home repository by day 60.
- Participation in code review (as reviewer) by day 60.
- On-call shadow shift completion by day 90 (for roles with on-call responsibility).
- Completion of mandatory security and privacy training (see [`10-training/`](../10-training/)).
