# ACME Corp Anti-Harassment Policy

This policy prohibits harassment of any kind at ACME Corp. It complements the [`01-hr/code-of-conduct.md`](./code-of-conduct.md) and is required reading for every employee. This is a fictional policy and not legal advice; real-world harassment policies require review by qualified legal professionals admitted to the relevant jurisdiction.

## Scope

This policy applies to:

- All ACME employees, contractors, interns, and temporary staff.
- All ACME offices, remote work locations, company-sponsored events, customer sites, and online spaces (Microsoft Teams, GitHub, email, forums).
- Any conduct involving an ACME employee, regardless of whether the other party is also an ACME employee.

## Prohibited Conduct

ACME prohibits the following conduct, whether intentional or merely having the effect of:

- Harassment based on gender, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, race, caste, religion, disability, age, national origin, marital status, pregnancy, or any other protected characteristic.
- Sexual harassment, including unwelcome sexual advances, requests for sexual favors, verbal or physical conduct of a sexual nature, and the creation of a hostile work environment.
- Bullying, including sustained verbal abuse, sabotage of work, public humiliation, or unreasonable exclusion.
- Retaliation against anyone who, in good faith, reports harassment or participates in an investigation.

The standard is **impact on the recipient**, not intent of the actor. A claim of "I was joking" is not a defense.

## Reporting Channels

Employees who experience or witness harassment should report through any of these channels — whichever they are most comfortable with:

1. **Their manager** — appropriate when the issue is not about the manager.
2. **Their HRBP** — see [`09-contacts/contact-directory.md`](../09-contacts/contact-directory.md).
3. **The HR Director for their office** — see [`00-company/organizational-structure.md`](../00-company/organizational-structure.md).
4. **The POSH Committee** (India) — `posh@acme.example`, monitored by the Internal Complaints Committee constituted under applicable Indian law.
5. **Legal and Compliance** — `hemant.joshi@acme.example`.
6. **Anonymous channel** — `speakup@acme.example`, monitored by Legal.

## Investigation Process

1. **Acknowledgement:** The recipient of the complaint acknowledges receipt within 1 business day.
2. **Interim measures:** Where appropriate, the Company may separate the parties (e.g., change reporting lines, suspend access, place one party on administrative leave) pending investigation.
3. **Investigation:** Conducted by an impartial investigator — typically HR Operations and, for India-based complaints, the Internal Complaints Committee. Investigations are typically completed within 30 business days.
4. **Findings:** Findings are documented in writing and shared with the complainant and the respondent.
5. **Action:** If a violation is found, disciplinary action ranges from formal written warning to termination, depending on severity.
6. **No retaliation:** Retaliation against the complainant or any witness is itself a violation of this policy.

## Confidentiality

Information about a complaint is shared only on a need-to-know basis. The complainant and respondent are both expected to maintain confidentiality to protect the integrity of the investigation and the privacy of all parties. Breaches of confidentiality may themselves be a disciplinary matter.

## False Complaints

A complaint made in good faith, even if not substantiated, will not result in any action against the complainant. A complaint made with malice and knowing falsity may result in disciplinary action.

## External Recourse

Nothing in this policy prevents an employee from seeking remedies available under applicable law, including approaching the relevant labor court or statutory body.
