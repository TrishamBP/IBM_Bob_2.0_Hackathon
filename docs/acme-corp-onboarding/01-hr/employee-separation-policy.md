# ACME Corp Employee Separation Policy

This policy governs all types of separation from ACME Corp — voluntary resignation, end of contract, performance-based separation, and role elimination. It is consistent with the offboarding workflows in [`12-offboarding/`](../12-offboarding/). This is a fictional policy.

## Types of Separation

| Type | Definition |
|------|------------|
| Voluntary resignation | Employee initiates separation. |
| End of contract | Fixed-term contract reaches its end date. |
| Performance-based | Initiated by the Company following unsuccessful PIP — see [`01-hr/performance-review-policy.md`](./performance-review-policy.md). |
| Role elimination / layoff | Initiated by the Company for business reasons, not individual performance. |
| Termination for cause | Initiated by the Company for serious misconduct (e.g., Code of Conduct violation). |

## Notice Period

- Notice periods are defined in the [`01-hr/sample-employment-agreement.md`](./sample-employment-agreement.md) and governed by applicable law. Standard notice:
  - India: 60 days (employee-initiated) / 60 days (Company-initiated, except for cause)
  - UK: 4 weeks (employee) / 4 weeks (Company, except for cause)
  - US: 2 weeks (employee) / 2 weeks (Company, except for cause)
- Notice can be mutually shortened by written agreement.
- For cause terminations do not require notice; they require documented investigation and Legal review.

## Resignation Process

1. Employee submits written resignation to their manager and HRBP.
2. Manager acknowledges within 1 business day and triggers the [`12-offboarding/employee-departure-checklist.md`](../12-offboarding/employee-departure-checklist.md).
3. HRBP issues a formal acceptance letter and a separation packet (final settlement, leave encashment, benefits continuation, exit interview).
4. The offboarding workflow in [`12-offboarding/`](../12-offboarding/) is initiated.

## Final Settlement

The final settlement includes:

- Salary through the last working day.
- Accrued and unused PTO encashment (per applicable law).
- Pending reimbursements.
- Bonus pro-rata (if eligible per plan terms).
- Statutory gratuity (India) where applicable.
- Severance (per individual agreement or per role elimination policy).

Final settlement is processed within 30–45 days of the last working day, depending on jurisdiction.

## Access Revocation

- Corporate identity (Microsoft Entra ID) is disabled at end-of-business on the last working day.
- Repository access (GitHub Enterprise) is revoked at the same time.
- Laptop and equipment return is governed by [`12-offboarding/equipment-return.md`](../12-offboarding/equipment-return.md).
- See [`02-it/it-access-revocation.md`](../02-it/it-access-revocation.md) for the technical process.

## Knowledge Transfer

- The separating employee completes a knowledge transfer (KT) plan with their manager.
- KT includes: handover of in-flight work, runbooks for owned services, customer and stakeholder introductions, and documentation updates.
- KT is documented in [`12-offboarding/knowledge-transfer.md`](../12-offboarding/knowledge-transfer.md).

## Exit Interview

- Conducted by HRBP or HR Director, not the direct manager.
- Topics: reason for leaving, manager effectiveness, role clarity, work environment, suggestions.
- Output is anonymized and aggregated quarterly for the leadership team.

## Non-Disparagement and References

- Both parties agree not to disparage the other.
- Reference checks are handled centrally by HR Operations; managers do not provide references directly.
- Confirmation of employment (dates and title only) is provided on request to any prospective employer with the employee's consent.
