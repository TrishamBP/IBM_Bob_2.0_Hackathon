# ACME Corp Office Locations and Working Arrangements

This document summarizes ACME's four offices, the working model for each, and the rules that apply in-office and remote. The governing policy is [`01-hr/remote-and-hybrid-working-policy.md`](../01-hr/remote-and-hybrid-working-policy.md).

## Office Overview

| Office | Address (fictional) | Headcount (approx.) | Time Zone |
|--------|---------------------|---------------------|-----------|
| Hyderabad (HQ) | ACME Tower, Hitec City, Hyderabad, Telangana 500081, India | 1,400 | IST (UTC+05:30) |
| Bengaluru | ACME Tech Park, Outer Ring Road, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560103, India | 900 | IST (UTC+05:30) |
| London | ACME House, 120 Bishopsgate, London EC2N 4AG, UK | 450 | GMT/BST |
| Seattle | ACME Westlake, 400 Westlake Ave N, Seattle, WA 98109, USA | 750 | PT (UTC−08:00 / UTC−07:00 DST) |

Each office has:

- A reception and security desk operational 24×7.
- Hot desks and bookable meeting rooms (booked via ACME Portal → Workplace).
- A lactation room, prayer/meditation room, and accessibility-compliant restrooms.
- An on-site IT Helpdesk point during business hours (see [`02-it/it-support-and-troubleshooting.md`](../02-it/it-support-and-troubleshooting.md)).
- A facilities contact (see [`09-contacts/contact-directory.md`](../09-contacts/contact-directory.md)).

## Working Models

| Model | Definition | In-office Expectation |
|-------|------------|------------------------|
| Hybrid (office-assigned) | Employee lives within commuting distance of an ACME office and is assigned to it. | Minimum two days per week in office, chosen in coordination with team. |
| Remote (domestic) | Employee lives in the same country as their hiring office but outside commuting distance. | No in-office requirement. Annual in-person gathering optional. |
| Remote (international) | Employee lives in a country without an ACME office. | No in-office requirement. Annual in-person gathering mandatory. |

Approval for remote status requires the hiring manager and the relevant HR Director (see [`00-company/organizational-structure.md`](./organizational-structure.md)). Remote employees must still comply with all security and equipment policies (see [`03-security/byod-policy.md`](../03-security/byod-policy.md) and [`02-it/device-acceptance-and-responsibility.md`](../02-it/device-acceptance-and-responsibility.md)).

## Core Collaboration Hours

Because ACME operates across IST, GMT/BST, and PT, we publish **core collaboration hours** that everyone is expected to be reachable within, with calendar boundaries respected outside them.

| Time Zone | Core Hours |
|-----------|-----------|
| IST | 09:00–14:00 |
| GMT/BST | 10:00–15:00 |
| PT | 09:00–14:00 |

Cross-region live meetings are normally scheduled between 18:00–21:00 IST (05:30–08:30 PT) and 16:00–19:00 BST (08:00–11:00 PT). No employee is required to attend a meeting outside their core hours more than two days per week.

## In-Office Etiquette

When you come to an ACME office:

- **Badging in is mandatory.** Your Microsoft Entra ID-issued badge opens all access-controlled doors. Do not badge in others (no tailgating). See [`03-security/acceptable-use-policy.md`](../03-security/acceptable-use-policy.md).
- **Hot desks must be booked** through Workplace on the ACME Portal. Unbooked desks may be reclaimed by Facilities.
- **Meeting rooms have a 15-minute grace period.** If you do not show up within 15 minutes of your booking start, the room is released automatically.
- **Visitors must be pre-registered.** Use the ACME Portal → Workplace → Visitors form. See [`09-contacts/contact-directory.md`](../09-contacts/contact-directory.md) for facilities contacts.
- **Confidential work in open spaces** is governed by the [`03-security/clean-desk-policy.md`](../03-security/clean-desk-policy.md). Do not leave customer data, code, or credentials visible on screens when stepping away.

## Remote Work Setup

Remote employees receive a laptop and an external monitor by default. Additional peripherals (keyboard, mouse, headset, second monitor, ergonomic chair) can be requested via the IT request process — see [`02-it/laptop-and-workstation-allocation.md`](../02-it/laptop-and-workstation-allocation.md) and [`08-forms/it-access-request.md`](../08-forms/it-access-request.md).

Remote employees must:

- Use the corporate VPN (see [`02-it/corporate-vpn-configuration.md`](../02-it/corporate-vpn-configuration.md)) when accessing ACME internal services.
- Encrypt their device (see [`03-security/device-encryption.md`](../03-security/device-encryption.md)).
- Store no customer data on local storage beyond what is required for active work, and never outside the corporate password manager and source control.

## Travel Between Offices

Employees traveling between ACME offices should:

1. Notify their manager and the destination office's HR Lead at least 5 business days in advance.
2. Book a hot desk at the destination office via the ACME Portal.
3. Carry their corporate laptop and badge; do not request a temporary loaner unless their device is being serviced.
