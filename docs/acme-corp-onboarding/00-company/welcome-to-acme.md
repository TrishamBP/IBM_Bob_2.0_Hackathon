# Welcome to ACME Corp

Welcome to **ACME Corp**, a fictional enterprise software company headquartered in Hyderabad, India, with additional offices in Bengaluru, London, and Seattle. We build cloud, AI, and productivity software used (in this fictional universe) by enterprises across the world. We are thrilled you have chosen to join us.

This welcome document is your first stop. It introduces ACME at a high level, points you to the people and systems that will help you in your first weeks, and sets the tone for the journey ahead. We have written every document in this library to be readable on its own — you should never feel lost — but this is the natural starting point.

## What ACME Builds

ACME develops three products:

1. **ACME Cloud** — an enterprise cloud management platform that lets customers provision, govern, and observe their multi-cloud estate through a single control plane.
2. **ACME Intelligence** — an enterprise AI platform that powers AI agents, model inference, and retrieval-augmented generation for customers in regulated industries.
3. **ACME Workspace** — a productivity and collaboration suite that combines documents, spreadsheets, video meetings, and project tracking.

See [`06-product/product-catalog.md`](../06-product/product-catalog.md) for the full catalog and [`06-product/acme-cloud-overview.md`](../06-product/acme-cloud-overview.md), [`06-product/acme-intelligence-overview.md`](../06-product/acme-intelligence-overview.md), and [`06-product/acme-workspace-overview.md`](../06-product/acme-workspace-overview.md) for the per-product deep dives.

## Who You Will Meet in Week One

You will be supported throughout onboarding by a small, named group of people. Your exact team depends on your role, but the following people are part of the standard onboarding experience:

- **Your hiring manager** — owns your onboarding plan and signs off on access requests.
- **Your onboarding buddy** — a peer from your team who helps you navigate day-to-day questions.
- **An HR operations specialist** — manages your employment records, benefits enrollment, and policy acknowledgements.
- **An IT onboarding specialist** — provisions your laptop, identity, and core software.
- **A security awareness trainer** — runs the mandatory security and privacy training.

You can find the canonical names and contact details in [`09-contacts/contact-directory.md`](../09-contacts/contact-directory.md).

## Culture at a Glance

ACME's culture, described in [`00-company/mission-and-values.md`](./mission-and-values.md), is built around five values: **Curiosity, Ownership, Rigor, Candor, and Care**. You will hear these values referenced in conversations, in design reviews, and in our internal recognition program. We do not list them as posters — we expect them to shape how we make decisions, especially when trade-offs are difficult.

## Hybrid and Remote Work

ACME operates a hybrid working model in Hyderabad, Bengaluru, London, and Seattle, and a fully remote model for employees outside commuting distance of those offices. The model is governed by [`01-hr/remote-and-hybrid-working-policy.md`](../01-hr/remote-and-hybrid-working-policy.md). Most teams expect at least two days per week in the office for employees assigned to an office location, with flexibility for personal circumstances arranged with the manager.
