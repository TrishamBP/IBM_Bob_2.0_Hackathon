## Role Categories

- `all` — applies to every employee
- `engineers` / `all-engineers` — applies to all engineering roles
- `managers` — applies to people managers
- `hr` — HR specialists
- `it-operations` — IT operations staff
- `information-security` — security staff
- `executives` — executive leadership
